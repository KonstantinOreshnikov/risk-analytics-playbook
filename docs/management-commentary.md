# Management Commentary

Good commentary explains what changed, why it matters and what should happen next.

## Four-part structure

1. **Observation** — the validated movement or comparison.
2. **Materiality** — why the movement matters.
3. **Driver** — a supported explanation, clearly marked when provisional.
4. **Action** — the decision, investigation or monitoring response.

## Example

> The metric increased for a second consecutive period and is now above its recent range. The movement is concentrated in one segment; other segments remain stable. The segment mix is a plausible driver but requires cohort-level validation. Review the affected cohort and confirm whether the change persists in the next refresh.

## Writing rules

- lead with the answer;
- include the comparison period;
- distinguish percentage points from percentages;
- avoid claiming causality from correlation;
- do not repeat every value already visible in the chart;
- state uncertainty explicitly;
- make the proposed action proportionate to the evidence.
